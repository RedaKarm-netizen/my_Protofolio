METHODOLOGY

How I Approach Problems

A systematic 6-step computational workflow from problem definition to deployed model.

01

Understand Problem

Identify the medical or biological objective, clinical parameters, and target outcome.

02

Collect & Clean Data

Wrangle clinical records or sequence files (FASTA/CSV), handling missing values and encoding features.

03

Analyze Data

Perform exploratory analysis, sequence alignment (BLAST), and statistical feature correlation.

04

Build AI / ML Model

Train classification algorithms (Random Forest, Scikit-learn) or bio-pipelines tuned for domain features.

05

Evaluate Results

Validate model accuracy, precision/recall, and generalization before deployment.

06

Deploy & Communicate

Build intuitive web interfaces (Streamlit) to present clear diagnostic risk insights.
